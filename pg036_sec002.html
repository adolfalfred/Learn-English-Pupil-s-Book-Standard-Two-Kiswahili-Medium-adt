I add sounds at the end of words or I finger spell letters at the end of words.

Activity 1

1. Pronounce or sign the words car , far and no . 2. Add a sound to the end of the words in (1). Or finger spell an additional letter to the end of the words in (1).

Example: car-card

3. Pronounce or sign the new words you have formed in (2). 4. Read aloud or sign the following sentences: (a) Every car has a card. (b) This tree is far from our farm. (c) No one will give a nod. (d) He parked a car near the cart. (e) There are ten people in the tent.
